Pick a city. Any city. Now ask yourself who runs it.

You will think of a mayor, a council, a bank tower with a famous name lit up at night. That is the answer you were trained to give, the way a dog is trained to look at the hand and not at the treat.

## Turn off the people for one day

Imagine the drivers do not drive. The nurses do not come in. The cooks, the cleaners, the teachers, the coders, the kid who stacks the shelves, all of them, stay home for a single day.

The tower is still lit. The name is still on the building. And the city is dead by lunchtime.

So who was holding it up?

> A throne is just a chair until a crowd agrees to kneel in front of it.

## The quiet arithmetic

Power has always depended on one simple trick: making the many forget that they are many. Keep them busy. Keep them tired. Keep them arguing with each other about things that do not matter, so nobody counts the room.

I am not asking you to march anywhere. I am asking you to count the room.

<span class="key">The establishment of a city is not a building. It is you, and the person next to you, and the person next to them.</span> You were the power the whole time. You just signed it over without reading the terms.
